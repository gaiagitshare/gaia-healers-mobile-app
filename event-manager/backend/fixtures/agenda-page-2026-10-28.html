About Dr. Jahani
Dr. Jahani holds a PhD with extensive research focused on the intersection of yoga and mental health, combining academic rigor with practical teaching and wellness practices. She brings over 15 years of professional experience in sound healing, yoga, Pilates, and breathwork — reflecting a deep commitment to holistic wellness and diverse fitness practices.
Certifications & Expertise
Certified Breathwork Practitioner — Expert guidance in breath control, relaxation, and energy management to support physical and mental well-being.
Certified Sound Healing Specialist — Trained in vibrational therapy to enhance healing, reduce stress, and promote emotional balance.
Holistic Fitness Approach — Integrates Pilates, yoga, breathwork, and sound healing to offer comprehensive fitness programs supporting both physical performance and mental wellness.
Teaching & Research
Successfully taught and mentored more than 4,000 students across various academic disciplines, adapting wellness programs to diverse learning styles and needs.
Her research includes in-depth studies on the impact of yoga, breathwork, sound healing, and fitness on mental health — including an evaluation of an Eight-Week Commercial Meditation Headband Intervention to Enhance Mindfulness and Reduce Stress Among Undergraduate Students.
