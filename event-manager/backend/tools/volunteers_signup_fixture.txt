Welcome, Gaia Healers Elevate Volunteers!

November 20–22, 2026 | Rosen Shingle Creek | Orlando, FL

Thank you for sharing your time, energy, and talents with the Gaia Healers Elevate Conference!

We are grateful for your willingness to serve and help create a welcoming, inspiring, and meaningful experience for everyone attending. Our volunteers are essential in bringing this event to life, and we couldn't do it without YOU!

Please note there are also opportunities on Thursday afternoon to help the leadership team ensure each area is ready to go on Friday morning.

Ready to Volunteer?

Please review the available volunteer opportunities and select the role(s), day(s), and time(s) that work best for your schedule. You are invited to sign up for additional shifts beyond the two 3-hour blocks per day throughout the conference.

A Few Important Reminders

Choose Your Role and Shift: Select the volunteer opportunities, dates, and times that interest you and that you can confidently commit to.

Arrive 20-30 Minutes Early: Please check in at the registration table before your scheduled shift. Arriving early allows time to connect with the volunteer finishing their shift, learn about your responsibilities, and ensure a smooth transition.

Honor Your Commitment: Your presence and punctuality help create a seamless experience for attendees, speakers, exhibitors, and fellow volunteers.

Bring Your Positive Energy and Love: A warm smile, kindness, and a willingness to help make a meaningful difference.

Communicate Changes: If your availability changes, please notify the volunteer team as soon as possible so arrangements can be made. Changes can be made to your shift schedule on this platform; please also send a text message to Bita as changes are made.

Conference Attire: Please wear a black or white shirt with coordinating bottoms during the volunteer shift. 

Volunteer Expectations: TWO 3-hour shifts a day are expected. You are welcome to sign up for more shifts as your schedule and desires permit. If you are not needed at your assigned location, please return to the registration desk for a different assignment. 

Together, we are creating an experience rooted in connection, community, and transformation.

Thank you for being part of the Gaia Healers Elevate community. We look forward to sharing this incredible experience with you!

Let's Elevate Together!
