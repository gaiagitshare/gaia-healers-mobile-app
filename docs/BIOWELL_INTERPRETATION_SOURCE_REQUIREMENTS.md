# Bio-Well interpretation: what we have, and exactly what we need

Purpose: one precise request to Bio-Well (and, where noted, to the Gaia
Practitioners platform team), so that any interpretation Gaia shows a member
rests on an authoritative source. Until an item below is answered, the app
shows the figure, its date and the member's own averages and differences,
and nothing more (see `READINGS_CLAIMS_INVENTORY.md`).

Wellness websites are not a source. Neither is anything a model writes.

## 2. Questions for Bio-Well

For each question we need the answer, the document it comes from (title,
version, date), and whether Bio-Well permits Gaia to show it to **members**
(not only practitioners).

### Energy

- **Q-E1. Definition.** What does the "Energy" figure measure, in plain
  language suitable for a member?
- **Q-E2. Unit and scale.** What are the unit and scale? Our UI says
  `J ×10⁻²`; an internal note says "joules ×100". Which is correct? Is 0–100
  the full range?
- **Q-E3. Ranges.** Does Bio-Well define any reference range? Today the app
  carried "most people sit between 40 and 70" with no source (now removed).
  If there is a range: for which population and device version, and may it be
  shown to members?
- **Q-E4. Variability.** How much does Energy vary between two scans of the
  same person on the same day, or on consecutive days? What size of change
  between scans is meaningful rather than measurement noise?

### Stress

- **Q-S1. Definition.** What does the "Stress" figure represent? Is it
  derived from the same fingertip images?
- **Q-S2. Scale.** Is 0–10 correct, and is it linear?
- **Q-S3. Ranges.** Does Bio-Well define a reference range? The app carried
  "2–4 comfortable; above 4 worth a conversation" with no source (now removed).
- **Q-S4. Variability.** Same as Q-E4, for Stress.

### Centres (chakras)

- **Q-C1. Value.** What does the per-centre `value` measure? Is "activity" the
  right word for members? What is its scale (our UI assumes 0–10)?
- **Q-C2. Alignment and asymmetry.** What are `align` / `alignment` and
  `asymmetry`, their scales and directions? Our explainer said "centred or
  pulled to one side" without a source (now removed).
- **Q-C3. Comparisons.** Is "highest" or "lowest" centre in one scan a
  meaningful comparison? Is the comparison between centres valid, or only
  within the same centre over time?
- **Q-C4. Interpretation.** Does Bio-Well publish an interpretation of a
  high or low centre value? If so, is it permitted for members, and in what
  wording?

### Balance and disbalance

- **Q-B1. Definition.** What is `disbalance` for organs, meridians and
  systems? What are its unit (we wrongly showed "%") and scale?
- **Q-B2. Threshold.** Is there a threshold above which Bio-Well itself calls
  an area out of balance?
- **Q-B3. Left/right.** What do `left` / `right` per area mean, and how do they
  relate to disbalance?

### Trends, comparisons and before/after

- **Q-T1. Trends.** What does Bio-Well recommend for comparing scans over
  time: minimum number of scans, time of day, conditions?
- **Q-T2. Averages.** Is averaging the latest three scans an acceptable way
  to summarise? Is a 90-day average?
- **Q-T3. Before/after.** How should a change between a scan before and after
  a session be described? (Lesson: "Why sometimes the after scan looks worse
  than before scan?") What must we never claim about it, for example that the
  session caused the change?
- **Q-T4. Rescanning.** What interval between scans does Bio-Well suggest for
  a person, if any? (Lesson: "How soon after a therapy should you scan a
  person?") Gaia uses 60 days as its own product policy for suggesting
  another scan, and will keep that wording unless Bio-Well defines one.

### Limitations and language

- **Q-L1. Limitations.** What are the official limitations: conditions that
  affect a scan (hydration, time of day, recent food or caffeine, calibration)
  and what a scan cannot show?
- **Q-L2. Approved wording.** What wording does Bio-Well recommend for
  describing results to clients?
- **Q-L3. Claims to avoid.** Which claims must not be made (diagnosis,
  treatment effect, disease, prediction) in each market we serve?
- **Q-L4. Device and software versions.** Do interpretations differ between
  Bio-Well 2.0 and 3.0, or between software versions? Is the version available
  in the scan data?

## 3. Questions for the Gaia Practitioners platform (partner MCP)

- **Q-P1. Flag rules.** What rule raises a flag, and how are `severity` and
  `direction` decided? Is the rule Bio-Well's own or the platform's?
- **Q-P2. Trend summary.** Is the 90-day `summary` (min, max, avg) computed
  over all scans or only complete ones?
- **Q-P3. Recommendations.** `get_customer_recommendations` is described as
  "AI-generated recommendations/scripts". Does the practitioner review or
  approve them before a member sees them? Is there an approved flag or a
  reviewed date we can check? Until there is, Gaia must not present them as
  "recommended by your practitioner".
- **Q-P4. Services.** Are a practitioner's `list_services` attributes
  written by the practitioner? (This would make R5 in the Path doc
  practitioner-authored.)
